In the assessment

A simple routine helps.

Good habits make it easier to show the mathematics you already know.

  1. 01Read

    Read the whole question and underline the important information.

  2. 02Work

    Show each stage clearly, even when using a calculator.

  3. 03Check

    Attempt every question, then return to anything unfinished.
